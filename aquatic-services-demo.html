Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new pond or aquarium cost?

It depends on the size, location, and equipment. We start with a consultation, give you a written price before anything is built, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and estimate are free.

Can you fix a pond or tank someone else built?

Yes. Restoration is one of our core services — leaks, green water, failing pumps and filters, or a system that's been neglected for years.

How often does a pond need service?

Most ponds do well with monthly service, while heavily stocked koi ponds and aquariums may need bi-weekly or weekly visits. We'll recommend a schedule after seeing your system.

Do you work on aquariums as well as ponds?

Yes — freshwater aquariums for homes, offices, and businesses, along with outdoor koi ponds and water features.

What areas do you serve?

The South Bay and surrounding Los Angeles communities, including Torrance, Carson, Gardena, Redondo Beach, Palos Verdes, and Hawthorne.
